# About This Book

I wrote *Agentic Journey* while building the systems described in these pages. It captures the agents and workflows we built and the people whose questions and experience shaped them.

Published November 18, 2026. This is a record of where the work stood when I wrote it.

## Reader analytics

This site counts visits to understand readership and which chapters people read. It uses a one-year anonymous browser cookie and records chapter visits, approximate IP-based city and country, browser and device type and referral source. IP addresses are retained privately for up to 30 days. Location estimates are obtained through IPWho.is, a third-party geolocation service. Anonymous visit histories and aggregated counts are stored in the site's private Render Key Value store. These counts are estimates and do not identify readers by GitHub account.
