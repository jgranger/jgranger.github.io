# Agentic Journey

We built our first AI agent to get some of our evenings back. At momoGood we were investigating messaging incidents after hours and wanted to do more of that work right in Slack. What started with one problem worth solving changed how I think about what people and agents can learn together.

In *Agentic Journey* I tell the story of how we built and ran these systems in production. An agent traces a production error back to the code and the decisions behind it. An automated evaluation gives us better scores while people get worse answers. A question an agent can't answer brings together colleagues who can. Each story turns on the same question. What do we need to know to make the next move? That’s what I mean by *operational context*.

Search gives us a way to find the evidence. Evaluation helps us see where the system falls short. Whether we learn from those failures depends on the people who notice them and what the organization does with what they know. When their corrections carry forward they give the next person a better place to start.
